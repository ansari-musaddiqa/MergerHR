About US

Merger HR Consulting is a unique HR Solutions firm providing quality services of recruitment (Permanent & temporary/contractual staffing), labour law/statutory compliances & blue-collar workers supply.
Merger HR Consulting was established in 2014 in Gujarat headquartered in Ahmedabad, with a purpose to serve MSME as well as big corporate companies for their talent acquisition& compliance related needs & challenges.
Our clients in manufacturing, IT, e-commerce, real estate, jewellery etc. industry have satisfactorily hired people through collaborative efforts with us.
Through in-depth understanding of vacancies and quick sourcing process, our team supply right candidates and close vacancies in a time-bound manner..

Vision :
To become all rounder HR Solutions company who can fulfil staffing & compliance needs of all types of industries for white-collar and blue-collar workers
Mission :
To serve Indian corporates for their manpower and allied needs with utmost satisfaction and cost-effectiveness.
